## the one sentence

atris takes custody of what you want and repays it with receipts you can
check, at machine speed, in your currency.

you say it badly at 3am. a system makes it precise, works it around the clock,
and shows you proof, not progress bars.

## the formula

prime intellect + polsia + cofounder + the simplicity of snapchat.

- from prime intellect: the market-maker shape. they aggregate distributed
  compute and verify the work. we aggregate distributed agent labor and verify
  the outcomes. same physics, one layer up: they sell flops, we sell results.
- from polsia: the promise. ai that runs your company while you sleep. we add
  the part that makes it believable: receipts you can audit when you wake up.
- from cofounder: the scope. every department, one platform: gtm, marketing,
  data, security, ops. we add the part that compounds: every function is a
  self-improving loop, not a task-runner.
- from snapchat: the interface. one gesture. you make a wish. everything else
  is the machine's problem.

## why we win: honesty is the product

every ai company promises outcomes. none can prove them.

atris is built on receipts: every claim traces to a verifier that is not the
thing that did the work. our own system caught its own test gate lying this
week and fixed it in hours. that discipline is the product.

the pitch to a founder: you do not have to trust me. check the receipts
yourself. if they are empty, stop paying.

## the machine underneath

- the folder: drop atris/ into any project and it connects. the brain is
  files, not a session.
- the loop: wish -> interview -> mission -> ticks -> receipts. runs on any
  frontier model; the models are workers, the loop is ours.
- the blocks: intake, adapter, verifier, cadence, receipt, policy gate, and a
  compiler. business capabilities are written as one-page manifests (packs),
  not built as codebases.
- packs: rainmaker (gtm), popshare (marketing), aeo, agentgrads, security,
  data. each one a self-improving business function under one umbrella.

## we can get data. we can build businesses.

- data: plain english in, real rows out of your postgres, rendered as living
  docs. the lakehouse experience without the data team. small businesses have
  gigabytes, not petabytes: that constraint is our price advantage.
- businesses: 80% of any company is repeatable machinery: funnel, launch,
  docs, outreach, support, billing. packs compile that 80% in days, so the
  founder only has to be right about the core 20%.
- proof by reflex: atris runs atris. the fleet that built this deck's claims
  landed them to master the same night, with tests, judged by a different
  intelligence than the one that wrote them.

## business model

one subscription. credits included. buy more credits when the machine works
harder. agents are metered compute; the pricing is the architecture.

each pack has its own front door (atris.ai/rainmaker) selling one named
metric to one niche. the interview is the sales call. the setup is the demo.
