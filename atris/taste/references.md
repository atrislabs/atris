## Braintrust (braintrust.dev), shown 2026-11-08

- **Take:** one sentence that states the problem and then the answer, with the answer in a quieter color.
- **Take:** art made from the product's own material. Their rows of marks look like a stream of logs.
- **Take:** three words in a loop to explain the product ("observe, evaluate, improve"). One loud button next to one quiet one.
- **Skip:** the all-caps side menu and commas before "and."
