# Taste

The short list of how Keshav likes things to look and read. Read this before you make anything a person will see.

Each line links to a note with the reason and real examples. Open a note only when your work touches it.

## The list

- One idea per screen, with one thing clearly in charge. [layout](layout.md)
- Giant type next to small quiet text. Nothing in the middle sizes. [layout](layout.md)
- Less text. A page should be understood at a glance. [copy](copy.md)
- Copy tells a story in plain connected sentences. No fragments like "Found today. Fixed today." [copy](copy.md)
- Never put a comma in front of and / but / so. Never use an em dash. [copy](copy.md)
- Every line must be true for every reader and make them feel strong, not wrong. [copy](copy.md)
- Buttons say what happens in the reader's words, like "Check my code." One button is the main one. [buttons](buttons.md)
- The art is made from the product itself, never stock or decoration. [layout](layout.md)
- No small labels above headlines, no all caps, no pulsing dots, no purple gradients. [avoid](avoid.md)
- Take the idea from a reference and make it ours. Never copy its lines. [references](references.md)
