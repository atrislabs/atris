# What Keshav loves

From the design taste test on 2026-11-07: 65 picks, six of them loves. Back to [TASTE.md](TASTE.md).

## The six

- **COS.** A white gallery page with sharp black type and huge editorial photos. His words: "Feels cinematic, sexy, less text. I can understand."
- **Waka Waka.** A stark editorial poster, like a printed sheet pinned to a wall.
- **Curater.** A welcome screen split into two calm halves, with the name doing all the work.
- **Melius.** A dark intro screen with one centered panel holding all the attention.
- **21 TSI.** A dark cinematic landing page with one full-bleed image in charge.
- **Leonid Kostetskyi.** A portfolio built almost only from oversized type on a warm paper color.

## What they share

One element dominates each screen. Each screen holds one idea with giant type sitting next to tiny text.

None of them explain much. You get it from the picture and one line.

## Also liked

Apple, Zara, Things, Linear, Headspace, Duolingo, Descript, Off-White, YSL. Most of these are restrained brands with a single strong move.
