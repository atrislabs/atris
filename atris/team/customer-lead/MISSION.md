## North Star

Every promise made to a customer is tracked, kept on time, or renegotiated before it slips.

## Score

Commitments kept on time out of commitments due. This needs customer data, so there is no score in this repo.

## One Tick

Read open commitments. Flag the nearest one at risk and draft the next step for a human to send.

## Stop

Stop at drafts. A human sends anything a customer reads.
