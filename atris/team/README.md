# Team

Who does what. Each line says what a member owns and when to open its file. Each member keeps its rules in `MEMBER.md` and may add `MISSION.md`, `SOUL.md`, `START_HERE.md` and a `skills/` folder beside it.

## Plan, build and review

- Turns messy intent into a precise plan with no loopholes. Open [navigator](navigator/MEMBER.md) when you need a plan before building.
- Builds from a spec one step at a time. Open [executor](executor/MEMBER.md) when you are the one writing the change.
- Checks the work, runs the tests and approves or bounces it. Open [validator](validator/MEMBER.md) when you review a change before it ships.
- The fresh-eyes skeptic consulted before a big commitment, never after. Open [architect](architect/MEMBER.md) before a migration, an API design or a refactor across many files.
- Turns project knowledge into one useful next task. Open [task-planner](task-planner/MEMBER.md) when the queue is empty.
- Turns broad intent into one bounded task and refuses to call it done without proof. Open [mission-lead](mission-lead/MEMBER.md) when you start or run a mission.
- The last check and the only voice that briefs the owner. Open [orb](orb/MEMBER.md) before anything reaches the owner's desk.

## Language, launch and customers

- Owns every sentence a person reads from the system. Open [linguist](linguist/MEMBER.md) when a status, card or message has to make sense to the owner.
- Takes validated work to docs, learnings and a published release. Open [launcher](launcher/MEMBER.md) after work passes review.
- Shapes raw ideas through visual and logical conversation. Open [brainstormer](brainstormer/MEMBER.md) when an idea is still fuzzy.
- Starts from what the customer is trying to achieve and keeps commitments. Open [customer-lead](customer-lead/MEMBER.md) when you handle a customer thread or promise.

## Research, security and engines

- Finds primary sources and says so when a claim cannot be verified. Open [researcher](researcher/MEMBER.md) when a question needs real evidence.
- Finds holes someone with less trust could use and proves each with a ledger. Open [sentinel](sentinel/MEMBER.md) when you run or judge a security audit.
- Keeps the engine roster true and every engine either working or marked down. Open [engine-manager](engine-manager/MEMBER.md) when an engine fails or a new one should join.
- Owns how videos become knowledge. Open [youtube-lead](youtube-lead/MEMBER.md) when you work on the video notes process.
- Brings in at least one outside lesson a week from tool releases, new models and papers. Open [scout](scout/MEMBER.md) when you look for outside ideas.

## Improving the system

- Ships one small verified improvement each tick with a receipt. Open [auto-improver](auto-improver/MEMBER.md) when you run the improvement loop.
- Suggests better member behavior without applying risky changes. Open [improver](improver/MEMBER.md) when a member keeps making the same mistake.
- Finds new features by combining proven ones and sends true gaps to the owner. Open [alpha-judge](alpha-judge/MEMBER.md) when you look for the next feature.
- Ships one durable overnight artifact per tick under a hard cost lock. It is experimental. Open [opus-overnight](opus-overnight/MEMBER.md) for overnight runs.
- A blank builder role. Open [builder](builder/MEMBER.md) only to fill it in.

New members start from [_template](_template/MEMBER.md).
