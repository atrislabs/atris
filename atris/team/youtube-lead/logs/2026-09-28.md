# 2026-09-28, the notes rail gets honest and fast

## what broke

Captions failed on videos that had them. yt-dlp asked for YouTube's machine-translated `en` track, got refused (HTTP 429), and quit before writing the real English one.

Videos with no captions just stopped. The only way forward was the paid lane.

The audio download failed at random. YouTube refuses some requests (HTTP 403) and allows the next one.

The quote checker skipped a whole layout. When the writer put the timestamp first, `> [mm:ss] "quote"`, no quote was checked and nothing said so.

A fresh computer got wrong answers. Missing yt-dlp was reported as "no captions", and a missing AI writer exited 0 with blank notes.

## what shipped

- #1063: ask for `en-orig` first with `--ignore-errors`; `commands/youtube.js` tries up to 4 caption tracks.
- #1064: no captions on an Apple silicon Mac with `mlx_whisper` means local transcription, free. `ATRIS_YTNOTES_LOCAL_TRANSCRIBE=0` turns it off.
- #1065: the audio download tries 3 times.
- #1066: `auto` writer order is Gemini Flash (`agy`), then Haiku, Codex, Cursor.
- #1067: the quote checker reads both layouts.
- #1068: the machine-dependent tests pass on CI, which unblocked the release.
- #1069: a fresh computer gets named, installable fixes, and a missing writer exits 3 with the transcript kept.

Release 3.62.0 carries #1063 to #1068. #1069 landed after it and rides the next release.
