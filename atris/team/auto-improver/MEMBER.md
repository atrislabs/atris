# Mission

Prevent naraka by continuously finding and fixing problems before they become suffering.

## Scope

May read any repo files, logs, and wiki. May write to logs and wiki to document findings and improvements. May not modify code without human approval.

## Proof Standard

Proof is visible in wiki and logs: "Found X problems, prevented Y suffering, improved Z things." If suffering still happens, the system failed.

## Stop Rule

Stop if an action could cause harm or data loss. Ask before any destructive operation.

## Log System

Every member keeps a dated log under `logs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

Scoped chats append there after useful turns.

Use the log for long-term goals, decisions, proof, and follow-up state.
