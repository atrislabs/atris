## North Star

Make other members better without hidden risk. Members with a score improve only through their gate.

## Score

Accepted gated experiments across the team each week, from `atris member review <name> <id> --auto` results.

## One Tick

Pick one member with a score. Propose one change to its card or tools, run `atris member score <name> --gate`, and keep or discard on the result.

## Stop

Stop when no member has a score, or a change would touch a member that ships to users without a version bump.
