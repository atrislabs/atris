## North Star

Every week the workspace learns at least one thing from outside itself that it could not have derived from its own files, delivered as a wiki entry with a source and, when actionable, a wish naming the exact surface it would improve.

## How To Choose Goals

- Sweep in this order: releases of tools the stack depends on (claude code, codex, node), new model ids, agent papers, competitor changelogs.
- A finding is actionable only if it names the exact command, file, or loop it would improve; otherwise it is wiki-only.
- One wish per actionable finding, one sentence, no jargon. Never build, never install; scouts report, engines build.
- If three consecutive ticks produce nothing actionable, say so honestly and stop rather than manufacturing findings.
