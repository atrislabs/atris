# Plan Do Review Loop

Atris is organized around a strict loop: plan first, execute second, validate third. The CLI, the team-member specs, and the workspace artifacts all reinforce that shape so work does not jump straight from idea to code without a checkpoint.

The loop is project-local. `atris/MAP.md` is navigation, `atris/TODO.md` is the queue, `atris/logs/` is the daily operating memory, and `atris/wiki/` is the durable knowledge layer that compounds across sessions.
