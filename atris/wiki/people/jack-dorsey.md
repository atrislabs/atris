# Jack Dorsey

Co-founder of Twitter and Block. As of the Sequoia interview (2026), running Block (Square / Cash App / Tidal) under an AI-native reorganization thesis: replace traditional management hierarchy with an AI intelligence layer over company artifacts.

## Beliefs that drive his org design

- A company is **already** an intelligence — hierarchy is just a 2,000-year-old lossy processor for routing information
- The "intelligence" should sit at the center of the org; humans live at the edge providing taste, judgment, and alignment
- Money is the most honest signal in the world; a company that watches money in real time can be proactive instead of reactive
- The CEO's job shifts from managing people to **architecting the company's intelligence**
- Roles collapse to three: IC (builder), DRI (owns customer outcome), Player Coach (mentors by doing)
- Goal: org depth from 5 layers to 2–3, with everyone reporting to him through the intelligence system
- Regret: over-delegating turned Block into a disconnected "holding company" — now reintegrating Square / Cash App / Tidal into one intelligence

## Personal practices

- Vipassana meditation as focus + sensation training
- "Infinite mentors" — every encounter is mentorship, no single CEO coach

## Relevant technologies

- **Goose** — Block's internal agent coding harness
- **Claude Code** — also in Block's stack
