# Closer to the Material — Ryo Lu (Cursor) | Compile 26

## Core Thesis

AI's value isn't speed-for-its-own-sake. It's collapsing the distance between
idea and reality so creators stay *inside the loop* — noticing, judging,
caring — rather than becoming passive approvers of "slop with working buttons."

## The Loop That Matters

```
try → world answers → notice what's wrong → adjust → build intuition
```

Craft lives in that loop. A black-box AI (wish → output → accept/reject) kills
it. A "glass" AI keeps it visible.

## Output vs. Material

- **Output** ends the loop — gives an answer, discourages tinkering.
- **Material** invites you back in — something to shape, own, think through.
  Code should be material, not output.

## The "Glass" Principle

> The system should let you see through its work.

Show plans, thoughts, tools in real time. Progressive disclosure: ignore the
details, or dive into infinite depths to steer or take over.

## Where Craft Moves

Craft doesn't vanish — it shifts to the **edges of judgment**:

- **Upstream:** what to ask for, what to keep, what to refuse.
- **Downstream:** taking responsibility for what ships — who it serves, what
  it changes.

## Key Quotes

- *"AI is collapsing the distance between idea and reality."*
- *"The loop matters because it keeps you awake. It forces you to notice. It asks you to care."*
- *"Output ends the loop. Material invites you back in."*
- *"Craft does not disappear. It moves to the edges of judgment."*
- *"The point of AI is not to make humans disappear. The point is to make more people feel — 'I felt this should exist, and I can make this real.'"*

## Relevance to Atris

The navigator→executor→validator loop, proof-on-disk, and the "don't be a
black box" stance in PERSONA.md all rhyme with Lu's "glass" principle — keep
the human in the loop, make the work inspectable, treat the artifact as
material. The glass run logs feature (CLI-337) directly implements this:
phase reasoning is persisted as material, not discarded as output.
