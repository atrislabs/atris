# Buttons

Back to [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

- The label says what happens in the reader's words. "Check my code" beat "Run the check" in the copy test.
- One main button per screen, in the one accent color. Others are quiet: flat, tonal, no border shouting.
- No glowing colored shadows, no two-color gradient fills, no hover lift.
- Full touch size on phones, at least 44 by 44 pixels, with a visible focus ring.
- The full button rules, including every state a button needs, are in [../design/SKILL.md](../design/SKILL.md).

Seen and liked: a solid main button next to a flat gray secondary one. One loud and one quiet.
