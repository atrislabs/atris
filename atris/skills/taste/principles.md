# General principles

Rules that hold for most readers. Use them when no taste file exists. Any taste file overrides them. Back to [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

## Words

- Tell a story in plain connected sentences. Lead with what it means for the reader.
- Every line must be true for every reader. A claim that fits only some readers makes the rest stop trusting the page.
- Make the reader feel strong, not wrong. Show what the product does well instead of what the reader lacks.
- Never claim something that has not been checked. If a feature is not built, say it is coming next.
- Do not explain the machinery. Say what the reader gets.

## Buttons

- The label says what happens, in the reader's words.
- One main action per screen. Every other action is quieter.

## Look

- One idea per screen with one thing clearly in charge.
- Art made from the product itself beats stock pictures and decoration.
- Take the idea from a reference and make it your own. Never copy its lines or its look.
- Avoid the tells that read as generated. The list is in [../../policies/ANTISLOP.md](../../policies/ANTISLOP.md).
