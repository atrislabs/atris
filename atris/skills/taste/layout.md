# Layout

Back to [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

- One idea per screen, with one thing clearly in charge. This is what all six loves share. See [loves.md](loves.md).
- Giant type next to small quiet text. Skip the middle sizes.
- Lots of empty space. Restraint reads as confidence.
- The art comes from the product itself. For Sentinel that means a map of every part of your code with the holes in red, never a stock picture or decoration.
- No big number with a small label under it unless the number means something on its own. A giant "4" on the Sentinel page failed with "wtf is that number."
- No grids of identical cards. No card inside a card.
- Motion plays once and means something. Nothing loops or pulses.
