# Index lines

How to write the line that points at a note. Back to [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

## Rules

- Lead with the rule or the answer in plain words. Many readers stop at the line and never open the note.
- Then say when to open the note, in the words a person uses mid-task: "Open this when you publish a release."
- Never use a bare topic name as the whole line. "loops: the loop system" tells the reader nothing about whether to open it.
- Keep an index to one screen, about 40 lines. When it grows past that, group lines under short headings or split an area index off.
- Two layers at most from boot. Boot files link to an area index and the area index links to notes. A note may link to a sibling note but should not be the only way to reach one.
- Do not stuff extra keywords into a line to win a search. Write what a person would actually look for.
