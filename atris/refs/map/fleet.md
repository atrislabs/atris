- `lib/fleet.js:647` checks credit failures first, then workspace and login refusals from the error field and the first five stderr lines before a tool transcript starts. Successful report and stdout text cannot trigger a rerun. Backup metadata keeps the refusal sentence for both receipt and printed handoff.
