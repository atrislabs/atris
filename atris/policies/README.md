# Policies

How finished work is judged. Each line gives the rule and says when to open the policy behind it. A skill says how to do a job and its policy says how to review it.

## Before anything leaves the building

- Never send raw source to a person. HTML, decks and screenshots need render proof and the exact recipient, subject and body shown before send. Open [outbound-artifact-gate.md](outbound-artifact-gate.md) before you email, post or share anything.
- Generic, hedging output fails. Cut the kill-list words, match the depth to the task and run the pre-flight checklist. Open [ANTISLOP.md](ANTISLOP.md) when you review any writing, code comments or plans.
- An essay or post needs a clear thesis, sections that move it forward and a read out loud. Open [writing.md](writing.md) when you review long-form writing before it is published.
- Messages from the day loop must come from real operator data and never guess mood from silence. Open [day-loop-voice.md](day-loop-voice.md) when you write morning, evening or ping messages to an operator.

## Building

- Keep backend code plain: names that show intent, abstractions that earn their keep and plain error handling. Open [atris-backend.md](atris-backend.md) when you build or review an API, service or data access.
- Every technical choice should be better, faster, stronger, simpler and cheaper. Each one leaves a receipt. Open [engineering-principles.md](engineering-principles.md) when you weigh an architecture choice or a new tool.

## How things should look and feel

- Avoid the generic generated look in fonts, color, layout and motion. Open [atris-design.md](atris-design.md) when you build or review a screen.
- The Atris visual identity in type, color, spacing and motion came from a design interview. Open [design-seed.md](design-seed.md) when you style an Atris surface.
- Advanced technology made calm: help without possession and proactivity that is earned. Open [product-design-principles.md](product-design-principles.md) when you decide how a product feature should behave for a person.
- Atris must protect the person who says it is wrong. Open [genesis.md](genesis.md) when a decision touches power, dissent or what Atris is for.

How the owner likes things to look and read lives in [../skills/taste/SKILL.md](../skills/taste/SKILL.md).
