# coach log

Observed from Ethan Ding, "why you can't copy palantir" (2026-08-12). Not a style to copy.

## what worked

- He lets the market argue first. A stack of confident, incomplete takes is more interesting than a thesis on line one.
- Category refusal is doing real work. Snowflake is a warehouse, McKinsey sells hours. Once those are named, "what is this actually" has somewhere to land.
- The definition is slightly ugly on purpose. Vertically integrated, outcome-based, software-and-services. A VC-friendly noun would have made the rest of the essay optional.
- The empty quadrant is the argument. Software companies protect margin. Consultancies protect hours. Palantir sits in the square both abandoned.
- Copycats fail by cloning a costume (FDE, ontology, deployco) instead of the coupled system.

## what not to take

- lowercase twitter cadence, `w/`, `bc`, namedrop density
- "I indexed every page" as the authority move
- writing our moneyball piece as a palantir clone with baseball swapped in

## if any of this is useful later

The moneyball sting is different: the roster is easy to copy, the discipline is not. That is an observation, not a rewrite instruction.
