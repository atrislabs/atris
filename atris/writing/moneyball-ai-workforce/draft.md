# The Moneyball AI Workforce

## The idea

An AI workforce should not choose one model and use it everywhere. It should define the role first, the proof for that role second, and the engine third.

## The claim to test

For each role, choose the lowest-cost engine that reliably meets the role's validation bar. Reserve high-cost models for the work that truly needs judgment, novelty, or taste.

## Open questions

- What is the right mathematical objective for cost, quality, uncertainty, and the cost of failure?
- How many receipts are needed before a lower-cost engine earns a role?
- Which tasks are genuinely high judgment rather than merely unfamiliar?
- How should a system escalate when confidence is low or validation fails?
