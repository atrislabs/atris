# field evidence for the moneyball claim (2026-08-12)

receipts from one day of building the system the essay describes. use freely, every claim below is verifiable in the repo.

## the scout run that proves the thesis

a cheap fast model (haiku tier) was asked to pre-gather code starting points for an expensive builder. it proposed eight citations. one was fabricated, a file:line that does not exist. deterministic verification checked every citation word-for-word against the actual files, deleted the fabricated one, and shipped the six that survived.

the cheap model hallucinated. the system made the hallucination unable to leave the building. that is the moneyball mechanic in one run: you do not buy a scout who never misjudges, you build a stat sheet misjudgments cannot survive.

## the referee data

every finished engine answer now gets graded by a cheap independent referee, verdicts append to one file, and a scoreboard reads pass rates per engine. first day of rows: grok and cursor each went two passes and one unsure on three real repo questions. one unsure was the referee breaking its own answer format, recorded honestly as unsure rather than invented as a grade.

answer to the essay's open question "how many receipts before a lower-cost engine earns a role": stop philosophizing, read the table. the table exists now and fills itself as a side effect of real work.

## the timeout numbers

the scout's first time limit was a guess: 45 seconds. three measured real runs took 59, 66, and over 150 seconds. the guess was killing nearly every real run while all synthetic tests passed. lesson for the essay: cost-quality decisions need measured distributions, not defaults, and the measurements come free once every run leaves a receipt.

## the honesty layer that makes cheap viable

same day, the dispatch layer was made unable to lie: a worker that commits nothing cannot print landed, an engine that exits silently becomes an honest no-output failure, killed jobs leave readable traces, stale claims get swept within the hour. cheap models are only cheap if you do not pay for their failures in silent wrongness. the honesty layer is what converts low cost per token into low cost per outcome.

## one sentence for the essay

the roster is rentable and churns weekly; the discipline is owned and compounds daily; whoever owns the discipline can always field the cheapest roster that clears the bar.
